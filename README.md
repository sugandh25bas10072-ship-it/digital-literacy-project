Digital Literacy-

Digital literacy is the ability to confidently and effectively use digital technologies such as computers, smartphones, and the internet. It includes skills like accessing, understanding, evaluating, and creating information using digital tools. A digitally literate person can communicate through emails and social media, use online platforms like GitHub, LinkedIn, and ResearchGate, and work with digital content such as documents, presentations, and code.
It also involves being aware of online safety, protecting personal data, and using technology in an ethical and responsible manner. Digital literacy is not just about technical skills but also about critical thinking and the ability to adapt to new technologies. In today’s digital age, it is essential for education, career development, and daily life, as most professional and social interactions happen online.

Task 4: Draft a Professional Email & Etiquette Guide

One important part of digital communication is understanding what should and should not be done on social media and in online interactions. In this task, I focused on creating a list of do’s and don’ts for responsible digital behavior.
Do’s include using respectful language, protecting personal information, and sharing accurate content. It is also important to maintain a professional tone and think before posting anything online. Don’ts include sharing fake information, using inappropriate language, oversharing personal details, engaging in online conflicts, and posting content that may harm one’s reputation.
Following these practices helps in avoiding misunderstandings and maintaining a positive digital image.
Learning Outcome: Through this task, I understood the importance of responsible digital communication and how proper online behavior can impact both personal and professional life.
